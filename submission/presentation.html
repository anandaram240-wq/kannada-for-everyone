Slide 2 • Problem Statement

The Real-World Language Barrier in Karnataka

Karnataka, home to Bengaluru — India's premier tech and educational capital — welcomes hundreds of thousands of new students, engineers, and professionals every year.

🚌

Daily Commute Friction

Commuters struggle to hail auto rickshaws, negotiate meters, confirm BMTC bus destinations, and handle change in crowded transit hubs.

☕

Everyday Living & Dining

Ordering food at self-service Darshinis, asking vegetable vendors for rates per kg, and discussing rent with landlords feels intimidating.

💼

Workplace & Social Bonding

Non-Kannada speakers often feel culturally isolated, missing casual cafeteria banter and the warmth of connecting in the local language.

Official Hackathon Objective:

"Develop an interactive platform that helps non-Kannada speakers learn practical Kannada through everyday conversations, situations, vocabulary, and guided practice."

Slide 3 • Market Analysis

Existing Systems & Educational Gaps

Traditional language tools fail beginner adult learners who need immediate conversational fluency.

❌

Limitations of Current Tools

  • • Isolated Vocabulary Lists: Memorizing words without knowing when or how to say them in a sentence.
  • • Academic Grammar Overload: Complex verb conjugations that intimidate complete beginners.
  • • No Real-World Context: Lack of practical scenarios like auto rickshaws, bus stands, and hospital visits.
  • • Sign-up & Paywall Barriers: Forced account registration before users can learn even a single word.
  • • Missing Phonetic Guidance: Beginners cannot read Kannada script without accurate transliterations.
✅

What Non-Kannadigas Actually Need

  • • Dialogue-First Learning: Hearing how Person A and Person B talk in authentic street settings.
  • • Situational Decision Training: Choosing what to say when an auto driver asks for double meter.
  • • Instant Audio Pronunciation: Web Speech API synthesis with clear Roman transliterations.
  • • Zero Friction: Open the browser and start practicing immediately without logins.
  • • Polite Cultural Etiquette: Knowing when to use "-ri", "-kodi" (respectful) vs "-kodu".

Slide 4 • Our Solution

Our Pedagogical Framework: The 4 Pillars

A structured, beginner-centric learning journey transforming hesitation into conversational confidence.

📖

1. Vocabulary

65+ high-frequency words across 12 categories with flashcards, examples, audio, and bookmarking.

💬

2. Dialogues

10 complete real-world conversations with Person A & B, line-by-line audio, and role-play mode.

🧭

3. Situations

10 real-life survival scenarios (auto, bus stand, Darshini, hospital) with interactive decision challenges.

🏆

4. Practice

Instant feedback quiz engine, streak tracking, unlockable achievement badges, and localStorage persistence.

Key Innovation: Authentic Spoken Kannada + Web Speech API + 100% Offline Persistence

Slide 5 • Implemented Modules

Core Functional Modules Walkthrough

All 5 core modules are genuinely functional, verified through 14 automated browser tests and live user interactions.

Module 1

Interactive Vocabulary

65+ curated entries across 12 categories. Includes real-time search, 3D flip flashcards, and "Mark as Learned" state.

Module 2

Real-World Dialogues

10 comprehensive everyday conversations (Auto, Darshini, BMTC, Clinic, Office). Line-by-line audio and comprehension check.

Module 3

Scenario Simulations

10 interactive situational scenarios with cultural etiquette tips, partner response expectations, and scenario decision quizzes.

Module 4

Quiz Engine & Review

Categorized tracks (Quick 5-min Sprint, Situational), instant pedagogical explanations, confetti celebration, and question-by-question review.

Module 5

Learner Dashboard

Weighted progress bar, daily practice streak counter, 8 unlockable badges, bookmarked words quick list, and reset modal.

Module 6

Phonetics & Web Speech

Kannada vowels (ಸ್ವರಗಳು), speed controls (0.7x, 0.85x, 1.0x), voice diagnostic check, and transparent educational fallback.

Slide 7 • Conclusion & Future Scope

Impact, Inclusion & The Road Ahead

"Kannada for Everyone" removes language anxiety, celebrates Karnataka's culture, and empowers newcomers to feel truly at home.

Demonstrated Impact

• Zero Friction Onboarding: Anyone can start learning in 3 seconds directly on their smartphone or laptop.

• Street-Smart Usability: Prepares learners for actual Bengaluru situations: bargaining, hiring autos, ordering dosas, and emergency calls.

• Promotes Harmony: Fosters mutual appreciation between native Kannadigas and non-Kannada speaking residents.

Future Roadmap

• Regional Dialect Explorer: Add North Karnataka (Hubballi-Dharwad) and Coastal Kannada (Kundapura/Mangaluru) variations.

• Speech Recognition Pronunciation Scoring: Enable users to speak into the microphone and receive instant pronunciation feedback.

• Offline PWA Support: Progressive Web App installable on Android and iOS devices with full offline caching.
