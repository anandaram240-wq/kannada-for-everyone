# Kannada for Everyone — 3 to 5-Minute Demonstration Video Script

**Event:** Kannada Digital Innovation Hackathon 2026  
**Organizers:** HackerRank Campus Crew & The National Institute of Engineering (NIE), Mysuru  
**Problem Statement:** Problem Statement #1 — Kannada for Everyone  
**Format:** Screen recording with voice-over (No face required)  
**Target Duration:** ~4 minutes (Range: 3 to 5 minutes)  

---

## Technical Recording Checklist for Presenter

- [ ] **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) or 1440p desktop browser window.
- [ ] **Zoom:** Set browser zoom to 100% or 110% for crisp typography and large Kannada font rendering.
- [ ] **Audio:** Clear microphone audio without background noise or room echo.
- [ ] **Browser:** Google Chrome or Edge with web server running at `http://localhost:3000`.
- [ ] **Drive Link:** Upload final `.mp4` / `.mov` to Google Drive and ensure link access is set to **"Anyone with the link can view"**.

### PART 3: LIVE PRODUCT DEMONSTRATION (1:15 – 3:30)

**Spoken Narration (Voice-over):**
> *"Let's explore our first core pillar: the **Kannada Vocabulary Builder**.*
> *We have curated over 65 high-frequency everyday words categorized into 12 practical daily categories: Greetings, Food, Transport, Numbers, Shopping, and Healthcare.*
>
> *Every card features authentic Kannada script, Roman transliteration, verified English meaning, and real-life sentence examples.*
> *Learners can switch between grid browsing and interactive 3D flashcard mode for active recall. Clicking the audio button plays speech synthesis to hear the exact phonetic cadence. Once mastered, a single click marks the word learned, immediately updating their persistent dashboard."*

## Recording Tips for Video Submission

1. **Audio Recording:** Use QuickTime Player on Mac (`New Screen Recording` -> Select Microphone) or OBS Studio.
2. **Cursor Visibility:** Keep your mouse cursor steady and deliberate when clicking buttons.
3. **Pacing:** Speak at a calm, enthusiastic, natural pace. The script is timed to complete comfortably in 4 minutes.
4. **Export:** Export in 1080p MP4.
5. **Google Drive Link:** Upload to Google Drive -> Share -> Change to "Anyone with the link can view" -> Paste into the Unstop submission form.
